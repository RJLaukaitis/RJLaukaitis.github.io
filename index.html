Frequently asked questions

Do I need to make an account?

No. Just open the app and start scanning.

Is it free?

Yes. You get 3 free scans every month. For unlimited scans, go Pro monthly or unlock it forever with a one-time purchase.

Does it keep my photos?

No. Images are processed to identify your tiles and aren't retained by TileWizard. See the privacy policy for full details.

What if the camera misreads a tile?

After every scan your tiles drop into a tray. Tap any tile to fix it, or add one the camera missed, before you see your hands.

How do I get in touch?

Email TileWizard@proton.me.
