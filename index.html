Tile Wizard Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Tile Wizard does not collect, store, or sell personal information from users.

Tile Wizard does not require you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or other personal information.

We do not use advertising trackers, analytics services, or third-party tracking technologies to build profiles of our users.

2. Photos and Camera Images

Tile Wizard allows you to take or select an image of your American Mah Jongg tile rack for the purpose of identifying tiles and analyzing possible hands.

When you submit an image for analysis:

  1. The image is securely transmitted for processing.
  2. The image is sent to OpenAI's API for AI-powered image analysis.
  3. The analysis is returned to Tile Wizard so the App can provide your results.
  4. Tile Wizard does not retain or store your submitted images.

Tile Wizard does not use submitted images for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than providing the requested tile-analysis functionality.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

Tile Wizard uses OpenAI to provide AI-powered image analysis.

Images submitted through Tile Wizard are processed by OpenAI's API. OpenAI states that data submitted to its API is not used to train or improve its models by default unless the customer explicitly opts in.

OpenAI may retain certain data for purposes such as abuse monitoring in accordance with its applicable policies. For more information, please review OpenAI's Privacy Policy and OpenAI's API data controls.

Tile Wizard does not control OpenAI's independent privacy practices.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

Tile Wizard may store certain app settings, preferences, or scan history locally on your device to provide App functionality.

This information is stored locally on your device and is not collected by Tile Wizard's servers.

5. Payments

If you purchase Tile Wizard through the Apple App Store, payment processing is handled by Apple. Tile Wizard does not receive or store your full payment card information.

Your purchase is subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

Tile Wizard does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because Tile Wizard does not require an account or intentionally collect personal information from its users, we do not maintain personal information about children.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information transmitted through Tile Wizard, including using encrypted connections when transmitting images for analysis.

However, no method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Tile Wizard does not maintain a database of user accounts, submitted images, or personal profiles.

Images submitted for tile analysis are processed for the requested functionality and are not retained by Tile Wizard after processing.

Third-party providers, including OpenAI, may have their own data retention practices. Their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies and service terms.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Because Tile Wizard does not maintain user accounts or retain personal information, there is generally no personal information held by Tile Wizard that can be accessed, corrected, or deleted upon request.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights under applicable laws.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

If we make changes, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this Privacy Policy.
