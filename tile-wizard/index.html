Description

From “what do I have?” to “what should I go for?” in seconds.

TileWizard is your American Mahjong coach. Point your iPhone at your tiles and TileWizard instantly shows the closest hands on the card, exactly which tiles you still need, and where your jokers fit. No sign-up, no menus to dig through — it opens straight to the camera.

How it works

1. Scan your rackLine your 13 or 14 tiles up in the guide and tap the shutter, or import a photo you've already taken.
2. Confirm your tilesEvery tile drops into a tray. Tap any tile to fix it or add one the camera missed.
3. Get your best handsSee the closest hands on the card, ranked by how few tiles you are from Mahjong.

Features

  • Know which hand to chase — checks your rack against every hand on the card and ranks the ones you're closest to, respecting joker rules and singles & pairs
  • See exactly what you need to win — tiles you have vs. tiles you need, joker eligibility for every group, and point values and concealed hands flagged
  • Friendly coaching — plain-language tips on why each hand fits your rack, for new and seasoned players
  • Track your game trends — log wins and losses, save favorite hands, revisit past scans, and watch your win rate and streaks grow
  • Color-blind-friendly suit colors and optional haptics
  • No sign-up, no account, no ad trackers
