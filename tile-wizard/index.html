Description

Stop squinting at the card. Let the Wizard read your rack.

Tile Wizard is a helper for American Mah Jongg players. Take a photo of your tile rack, and Tile Wizard identifies your tiles and shows you which hands you're closest to — so you can spend less time counting and more time playing.

How it works

1. SnapTake a picture of your rack, or pick one from your photos.
2. IdentifyTile Wizard recognizes each tile in the image.
3. PlaySee the hands that fit your tiles and decide what to keep.

Features

  • Photo-based tile recognition for American Mah Jongg sets
  • Hand analysis to show which hands you can go for
  • No sign-up, no account, no ad trackers
  • Settings and history stay on your device

Privacy
