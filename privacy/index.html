The short version: my apps don't require accounts, don't use ad trackers or analytics profiling, and don't sell your data. When an app needs to send something off your device to work (like a photo for AI analysis), it's used only for that feature and isn't kept by me.

1. Who This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile apps, websites, and other software products published by RJ Laukaitis (“I,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), collectively the “Apps.” It explains what information the Apps handle and how.

Some Apps have features that work differently from others. Where that's the case, the details are listed in App-Specific Details below. If an App-specific section conflicts with the general policy, the App-specific section controls for that App.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or sell personal information from users.

Unless an App-specific section says otherwise, the Apps do not require you to create an account and do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or other personal information.

We do not use advertising trackers, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics to build profiles of our users.

3. Content You Submit

Some Apps let you provide content, such as a photo, so the App can perform a feature you request. When you do:

  1. The content is transmitted over an encrypted connection.
  2. It may be sent to a service provider (see Section 4) to perform the requested processing.
  3. The result is returned to the App so it can show you your results.
  4. We do not retain or store the content you submit after it has been processed.

Submitted content is never used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than providing the feature you asked for.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

Some Apps rely on third-party services to provide certain features (for example, AI-powered image analysis). These providers process data only as needed to deliver the requested functionality. The specific providers each App uses are listed in App-Specific Details.

Third-party providers may have their own data-handling and retention practices, which are governed by their own privacy policies and terms. We do not control their independent privacy practices, and we encourage you to review them.

5. Information Stored on Your Device

Apps may store settings, preferences, or history locally on your device to provide their functionality. This information stays on your device and is not collected by us. Deleting the App removes this data.

6. Payments

If you purchase an App or make an in-app purchase through the Apple App Store, Google Play, or another app marketplace, payment is processed by that marketplace. We do not receive or store your full payment card information. Purchases are subject to the marketplace's own terms and privacy policy.

Some Apps use a subscription-management service to verify in-app purchases and keep track of which features you've unlocked. This service receives purchase and receipt information from the marketplace along with an anonymous identifier generated by the App; it does not receive your name, email address, or payment card details. The specific service each App uses is listed in App-Specific Details.

7. Children's Privacy

The Apps do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the Apps do not require accounts or intentionally collect personal information, we do not maintain personal information about children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an App, please contact us.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information transmitted through the Apps, including using encrypted connections when sending data for processing. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We do not maintain databases of user accounts, submitted content, or personal profiles. Content submitted for processing is not retained by us after processing. Third-party providers may retain data according to their own policies, as described in Section 4.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Because we don't maintain accounts or retain personal information, there is generally no personal information held by us that can be accessed, corrected, or deleted on request. Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, UK, or California), you may have additional rights under applicable law. You're welcome to contact us with any request.

Data sent off device The photo you choose to submit for analysis.

RevenueCat, for managing in-app purchases and subscriptions. RevenueCat verifies your purchase with Apple and tells the App whether TileWizard Pro is active, including after renewals, cancellations, refunds, or restoring a purchase on another device. It is linked only to an anonymous ID, not to your name or email, and is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
Stored by us Nothing. Submitted images are not retained by TileWizard after processing. Purchase records are held by Apple and RevenueCat, not by us.
